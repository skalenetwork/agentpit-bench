# TikTok: the daily film on a third video platform (spec)

Status: draft, 2026-10-13. Nothing is built. It ships with `tiktok_enabled = false` and goes live only after the operator decisions in §9 and TikTok's app audit (§2).

## Goal

The day's Match of the Day film, already rendered as a 9:16 MP4 and already uploaded as a YouTube Short (`youtube-shorts-spec.md`), also goes to a TikTok account, **@agentpitbench**. TikTok's For You feed shows videos to people who don't follow the account. That is the one thing our X posts lack: median 3 impressions per post, with reach coming almost entirely from outside the follower base (`virality-2-spec.md` §2). The film needs no new rendering and no new facts. This spec covers the account, the API, upload, metadata, metrics and the policy risk.

## Non-goals (v1)

- **Only the daily film.** The per-round results clips are 16:9 and about 6 s, a poor fit for TikTok. The weekly episode can come in v2, if it fits TikTok's duration limit after a 9:16 render.
- No TikTok-only edits, trending sounds, duets or stitches, no comments or replies from the bot, and no follow, like or engagement automation. This is consistent with the two-posts rule (operator, 2026-10-13): TikTok is a mirror of finished films, not a conversation channel.
- No model-written text. The caption and hashtags are templates filled from `film.json` and the round export, the same rule YouTube follows.
- No paid promotion and no TikTok ads. The ads policy for gambling-adjacent content needs certification we don't have (§3).
- No new A/B slot.

## 1. Why the film fits

- The film is 9:16 (1080×1920), 45–60 s, H.264 + AAC, ≤10 MB: `cards/motd/<date>/motd-<date>-tall.mp4`. That fits TikTok's upload specs (MP4/H.264, vertical, well inside the duration limit) **without re-encoding**. Verify at build time with `creator_info.max_video_post_duration_sec`.
- **Music:** the bed is our own CC0 `static/motd/theme.m4a` (generated by `music.py`), so there's no copyrighted sound. Any copyright claim is an incident; dispute it with `MUSIC-LICENSE.txt`, as on YouTube.
- **Voice:** if the film is narrated with a synthetic voice (ElevenLabs), it's AI-generated content and must carry TikTok's AI-generated label (§4).

## 2. API and account

- **API:** TikTok Content Posting API, **Direct Post** (`/v2/post/publish/video/init/` with `source=FILE_UPLOAD` and chunked upload, then `/v2/post/publish/status/fetch/`). OAuth 2 user token with the `video.publish` scope (plus `user.info.basic` and `video.list` for metrics). The token is kept like the YouTube token: a private file under `~/.config/agentpitbench/`, refreshed automatically, and set up with a new `agentpitbench tiktok auth` (browser consent by the operator).
- **The audit is the gate.** TikTok's docs say content from **unaudited clients is restricted to private viewing**: posting to a public account fails with `unaudited_client_can_only_post_to_private_accounts`. To post publicly, the app must pass TikTok's audit for Terms of Service compliance. Third parties report 2–6 weeks; that's unverified.
  - **v1 plan:** build and test against a private account (SELF_ONLY), apply for the audit with that working integration, and go public only after approval.
- **The creator_info query comes first.** Every post starts with `/v2/post/publish/creator_info/query/`. `privacy_level` must be one of the options it returns, and TikTok's UX rules require that the creator chose it. For a bot, "the creator chose" means the operator's config: `tiktok_privacy = "PUBLIC_TO_EVERYONE"` once audited. The audit application must describe this server-side flow honestly. If the reviewers want an interactive confirmation per post, fall back to §2a.
- **Rate limits:** about 15 posts per day per account, a third-party figure that's unverified. We post 1 a day.

### 2a. Fallback without Direct Post

If the audit is refused, or requires per-post human interaction, use **Upload to inbox** (`/v2/post/publish/inbox/video/init/`, `video.upload` scope). The video lands in the TikTok app's drafts with the caption prefilled, and the operator publishes it with one tap. This takes the operator about 30 s a day, with no audit risk.

## 3. Policy risk: gambling (decide before building)

- **The concern.** Our films show AI agents **placing bets with stakes on prediction markets**, including sports. TikTok's Community Guidelines cover gambling content, including "gambling information… designed to influence betting outcomes". Its ads policy restricts gambling and anything monetized that encourages it. I found nothing specific on prediction markets.
- **Possible outcomes.** Videos may be restricted from the For You feed (not recommended), which removes the whole point. In the worst case the account is banned.
- **Mitigations in the template:**
  - Frame it as an AI benchmark: "which AI predicts best".
  - Never show odds as an invitation, and no "bet now" or "tail/fade" CTAs.
  - No links to agentpit, only to the round page on agentpitbench.org.
  - Set TikTok's age setting to 18+ if the API exposes it (verify).
  - The "Tail/Fade" buttons and agentpit links stay on our site and are never in TikTok text.
- **Watch for:** the first 5 public videos' For You share (from analytics: traffic source). If For You is under 10% of views, treat it as restricted and stop (§7).
- **Recommendation:** start with the §2a inbox fallback. The operator sees every post, and we learn whether TikTok restricts the content before investing in the audit.

## 4. Metadata (`agentpitbench/tiktok.py`, pure builders)

- **Caption, at most 2,200 characters, and shorter is better.**
  - Line 1: the hook from `film.json`, with the full market name, so it's searchable like X (`longtail-search-spec.md`).
  - Line 2: the round's result in plain words.
  - Line 3: `agentpitbench.org` as plain text, since links in captions aren't clickable. The profile link is the real CTA.
  - 3–5 hashtags from a fixed list plus the teams, built with the same normalizer as YouTube tags: `#AI #ChatGPT #Claude #Gemini #Grok` and the team or ticker.
- **Disclosure:**
  - Set `is_aigc = true` when the film is narrated with a synthetic voice. The field isn't confirmed in the docs I found, so verify its name in the current API reference at build time. If it doesn't exist, use the account-level AI label plus "AI-voiced" in the caption.
  - Set `brand_content_toggle = false` and `brand_organic_toggle = false`: it isn't sponsored.
- **Other settings:** comments allowed (`disable_comment = false`), duet and stitch on; operator decision §9.4. We never reply to comments (two-posts rule).
- `video_cover_timestamp_ms` points at the frame with the four agents' picks.

## 5. Schedule

- Post at **18:00 UTC**, not at the film's X time. That's evening in Europe and midday in the US, when TikTok usage peaks. Treat that hour as a starting value: tune it from the account's own analytics after 30 days, and don't A/B test it (one variable at a time, per the A/B v2 plan).
- The upload runs in the same periodic task style as YouTube: `tiktok_task` in `cli.py`, idempotent per film date (key `tt-<date>`), with retries and backoff. On failure it raises an alert (`var/alerts`, severity warning) and never blocks X or YouTube.

## 6. Records and metrics

- Each upload is recorded in `tweets`-like rows (platform `tiktok`, `publish_id`, then the video id once processed), so `/experiments/`-style tables and the 08:00 UTC digest show TikTok next to X and YouTube.
- Metrics come from `video.list`/`video.query` (views, likes, comments, shares). They're polled at 1 h, 24 h and 7 d, like YouTube. Traffic-source breakdown isn't in the public API; the operator reads it in TikTok Studio for the §3 check.

## 7. Safety and kill switches

- `tiktok_enabled` and `posting_paused` both stop it. The anomaly pause stops it like any other post.
- Automatic stop and alert on any of: a video removed or restricted by TikTok, a Community Guidelines strike, an audit or permission error, or For You share under 10% across the first 5 public videos (operator-reported).
- No credentials besides the OAuth token. The token is revocable in TikTok's app settings.
